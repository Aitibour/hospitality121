This recognition is based on a comprehensive study that evaluated guest satisfaction across six key dimensions. With 583 properties open nationwide, WoodSpring Suites has demonstrated its commitment to delivering exceptional guest experiences. The brand's success can be attributed to its focus on providing clean, comfortable, and affordable accommodations to extended stay guests.
