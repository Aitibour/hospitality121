The luxury hotel industry is set to converge in Orlando this December for a major summit. The event, which will take place on December 15-16, will bring together approximately 900-1,000 luxury hotel executives. Keynote speakers will include prominent industry figures, such as Marriott's Tina Edmundson and Club Med's Carolyne Doyon. These speakers will share insights on various topics, including investment strategies, the role of artificial intelligence, wellness initiatives, and food and beverage strategy. 
   
   The summit will provide a platform for luxury hotel executives to network, share knowledge, and discuss the latest trends and challenges in the industry. With the luxury hotel sector experiencing significant growth, this event is expected to be highly relevant and informative. 
   
   Investment in the luxury hotel sector is a key area of focus, with many executives looking to expand their portfolios and explore new opportunities. The summit will provide valuable insights into the latest investment trends and strategies, helping executives to make informed decisions. 
   
   The role of artificial intelligence in the luxury hotel sector is also a major topic of discussion. Many hotels are now using AI to enhance the guest experience, improve operational efficiency, and drive revenue growth. The summit will explore the latest AI trends and technologies, and how they can be applied in the luxury hotel sector. 
   
   Wellness initiatives are another important area of focus for luxury hotels. With many guests seeking healthy and wellness-focused experiences, hotels are responding by offering a range of wellness programs and amenities. The summit will examine the latest wellness trends and strategies, and how they can be integrated into hotel operations. 
   
   The food and beverage sector is also a critical component of the luxury hotel experience. The summit will explore the latest F&B trends and strategies, including the use of local and sustainable ingredients, and the importance of creating unique and memorable dining experiences. 
   
   Overall, the luxury hotel summit in Orlando is expected to be a highly informative and engaging event, providing valuable insights and networking opportunities for luxury hotel executives. The event will help to shape the future of the luxury hotel industry, and provide a platform for executives to share knowledge, ideas, and best practices.
