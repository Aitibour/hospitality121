A coalition of major hotel associations from the United States, Canada, and Mexico is calling for a long-term extension of the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA). This move is driven by the significant economic benefits the agreement brings to the hospitality industry across North America. The hotel industry in these countries supports over 9 million jobs and generates more than $176 billion in visitor spending annually. 
   
   The USMCA has played a crucial role in facilitating travel and trade between the three nations, thereby boosting hotel occupancy rates and revenue. A long-term extension of the agreement would provide stability and predictability for hotel owners, operators, and investors, enabling them to make informed decisions about their businesses. 
   
   The economic impact of the hotel industry is substantial, with hotels serving as significant employers and contributors to local economies. By supporting the extension of the USMCA, the hotel coalition aims to ensure the continued growth and prosperity of the industry. 
   
   For hotel operators, a stable trade environment is essential for planning and investment. The USMCA's provisions related to travel and tourism have helped increase visitor numbers and spending, benefiting hotels and related businesses. 
   
   The coalition's call for a long-term USMCA extension reflects the industry's desire for a predictable and stable trade environment. This would allow hotels to focus on providing high-quality services to their guests, while also contributing to the economic development of their respective countries. 
   
   In addition to job creation and economic growth, the hotel industry also plays a vital role in promoting cultural exchange and understanding between nations. By facilitating travel and tourism, hotels help bridge cultural divides and foster greater cooperation between the United States, Canada, and Mexico. 
   
   As the hotel industry continues to recover from the impacts of the pandemic, a long-term USMCA extension would provide a welcome boost to the sector. With its emphasis on trade, travel, and economic cooperation, the agreement has the potential to drive growth and prosperity for hotels and related businesses across North America.
