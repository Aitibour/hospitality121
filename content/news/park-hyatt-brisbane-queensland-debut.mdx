## Park Hyatt Brisbane Announcement
Hyatt has confirmed the launch of the brand’s first Park Hyatt in Queensland. The new property will be situated on the Brisbane Riverfront and is planned to open in 2031. The hotel will comprise 201 guest rooms and suites, offering the brand’s signature luxury experience. Development will be undertaken in partnership with the Shayher Group, which will act as the local developer and operator collaborator. The announcement was released on 18 November 2026 and marks Hyatt’s entry into the Queensland luxury market.
