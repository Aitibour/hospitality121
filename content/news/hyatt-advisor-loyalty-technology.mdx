Hotel loyalty technology also serves the people who recommend a property. Hyatt's Inclusive Collection provides an example through Confidant Rewards, which connects travel-advisor incentives with bookings and professional education.

An October 30 report in Hotel Technology News brought the programme's Antavo-powered platform back into view. This is a case-study analysis, not a new product announcement: Antavo dates the revamped programme's launch to 2023.

## What the programme connects

Hyatt's own travel-advisor site describes a combination of training, rewards and benefits. Advisors can qualify for privileges through learning and production, with rewards including personal stays and cash incentives. This is a programme for the advisor channel, rather than a replacement for guest loyalty.

Antavo describes technology that supports segmentation, tier progression and targeted campaigns. Its case study reports a 43% increase in bookings claimed through the programme and a 42% rise in associated revenue, comparing 2024 with 2023. Those are supplier-reported programme measures. They do not establish an equivalent increase across Hyatt's business or isolate the platform's effect from other changes. [Antavo's account](https://antavo.com/blog/confidant-rewards-loyalty-program/) and [Hyatt's advisor information](https://www.hyattinclusivecollection.com/en/travel-advisors/) provide the underlying descriptions.

## Hospitality121 analysis: the booking record is the foundation

A reward creates a promise to an advisor. Before an operator launches an incentive, it needs a consistent answer to a practical question: which booking earns what benefit, and when?

Consider a reservation that is amended after points are awarded. A reliable workflow would reconcile the change, explain the adjustment and give the advisor a route to challenge an error. Otherwise, a campaign designed to strengthen the relationship can create extra administration for sales and support teams.

Training adds another dependency. If eligibility requires a course, the learning record and loyalty record need to agree. The team should test delayed updates, duplicate accounts and bookings submitted through different channels before expanding the offer. These are proposed checks for operators, not reported faults in Hyatt's programme.

## Measure the incentive as well as the activity

For a hotel group evaluating a similar approach, programme participation is a useful starting point. The more demanding questions concern cancelled stays, reward costs, support workload and whether an incentive changes the business the hotel actually retains.

A practical pilot could begin with one clearly defined advisor offer and a documented reconciliation process. Compare eligible bookings with completed stays, track disputes and record the time needed to resolve them. These suggested measures would help distinguish an attractive campaign from a dependable distribution tool.
