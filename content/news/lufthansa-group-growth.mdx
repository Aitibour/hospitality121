The Lufthansa Group is looking to expand its operations in key European cities, with a focus on Rome and potentially Lisbon. However, the group's success is largely dependent on the performance of its Swiss subsidiary. Swiss has been a standout performer for the group, with its Zurich hub serving as a model for efficiency and profitability. 
   
   The challenge for the Lufthansa Group is to replicate this success in other markets. The group's German operations have faced significant challenges in recent years, including increased competition and rising costs. As a result, the group is looking to its other subsidiaries, including Swiss, to drive growth and profitability. 
   
   The expansion into Rome and Lisbon is a key part of this strategy. Both cities offer significant opportunities for growth, with large and underserved markets. However, the group will need to navigate complex regulatory environments and intense competition to succeed.

Hotel brands can learn from this approach, by focusing on building strong relationships with customers and delivering consistent, high-quality experiences.
