The World Cup has been a significant event for the travel industry, with hotels and short-term rentals experiencing real gains. The event has driven up rates, resulting in increased revenue for these sectors. However, the impact on US airlines has been minimal, with little to no noticeable increase in bookings or revenue. 
   
   The international visitor boom that was expected to accompany the World Cup has also fallen short of expectations. This is likely due to a range of factors, including economic uncertainty and changing travel habits. Despite this, the World Cup has still provided a welcome boost to the hospitality industry, particularly in terms of hotel bookings and occupancy rates.
   
   Hotel owners and operators have been working hard to capitalize on the World Cup, offering special packages and promotions to attract fans. This has included everything from themed rooms to exclusive access to World Cup events. The results have been positive, with many hotels reporting increased occupancy and revenue during the tournament.
   
   The impact of the World Cup on the hospitality industry extends beyond just hotels and short-term rentals. The event has also driven up demand for food and beverage services, as well as other tourism-related activities. This has provided a welcome boost to local economies, particularly in areas surrounding World Cup venues.
   
   Despite the positive impact on hotels and rentals, the lack of impact on US airlines is a concern. This may be due to a range of factors, including the increasing popularity of alternative modes of transportation, such as trains and buses. It may also be related to changing travel habits, with more people opting for shorter, more local trips rather than long-haul flights.
   
   The World Cup has also highlighted the importance of effective distribution strategies in the hospitality industry. Hotels and short-term rentals that have been able to effectively market and distribute their properties have seen the greatest gains. This includes using online travel agencies, social media, and other digital channels to reach potential customers.
   
   Overall, the World Cup has provided a welcome boost to the hospitality industry, particularly in terms of hotel bookings and occupancy rates. While the impact on US airlines has been minimal, the event has still driven up demand for a range of tourism-related activities, providing a welcome boost to local economies.
