NoVacancy Asia has published an updated agenda for its November 24 and 25 event at Bangkok's Queen Sirikit National Convention Centre. The organizer lists eight content tracks, including Hotel Technology, Revenue and Distribution, Guest Experience, and Independent Hotels.

The technology programme includes sessions on practical hotel uses of AI, connecting commercial data, moving technology from rollout into daily operations, and the effect of AI on booking and distribution. Other sessions address payment design, direct booking, guest-data silos and technology choices for independent properties.

The agenda is a programme announcement, not evidence that any featured method or product has delivered a verified result. Session details and speakers can also change before the event.

## Turn conference themes into decisions

Hospitality121 analysis: hotel teams can get more value from a broad agenda by arriving with one defined operating problem. A property might choose a slow manual workflow, a fragmented guest-data handoff or a booking-stage payment issue, then compare sessions and suppliers against that same problem.

After each discussion, record the required systems, data, owner and success measure. This makes it easier to distinguish a useful operating proposal from a demonstration that depends on data or integrations the hotel does not have.

## Questions to carry into the room

- Which hotel system is the source of truth for the proposed workflow?
- What staff action remains when the automation is uncertain?
- How is guest consent handled when data moves between platforms?
- What measurable baseline should be captured before a pilot begins?
