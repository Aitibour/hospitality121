A recent campaign by Holiday Inn Express has shed light on the morning routines of people in the UK and Ireland. The study, which was conducted in partnership with comedian Rob Beckett, aimed to understand the wake-up rituals that define the region. The research found that people in the UK and Ireland have unique preferences when it comes to waking up, with many prioritizing a good night's sleep and a hearty breakfast. 
   
   The study's findings have significant implications for the hospitality industry, particularly when it comes to catering to the needs of guests. Hotels can use this information to tailor their services and amenities to meet the demands of their guests, such as providing a wide range of breakfast options or offering relaxing spaces to help guests unwind in the morning. 
   
   The research also highlighted the importance of a comfortable and restful sleep environment, with many respondents citing a good night's sleep as essential for starting their day on a positive note. This underscores the need for hotels to invest in high-quality bedding and pillows, as well as to create a peaceful and quiet atmosphere in their guest rooms. 
   
   In terms of breakfast preferences, the study found that people in the UK and Ireland have diverse tastes, with some preferring traditional breakfast items like eggs and bacon, while others opt for healthier options like fruit and yogurt. Hotels can use this information to create breakfast menus that cater to a wide range of tastes and dietary requirements. 
   
   The campaign by Holiday Inn Express is a great example of how hotels can use research and data to inform their services and improve the guest experience. By understanding the needs and preferences of their guests, hotels can create a more personalized and welcoming environment, which can lead to increased customer satisfaction and loyalty. 
   
   The study's findings also have implications for hotel marketing and branding, as hotels can use the research to create targeted campaigns and promotions that resonate with their target audience. For example, hotels could create social media campaigns that highlight their breakfast options or promote their comfortable and restful sleep environment. 
   
   Overall, the research by Holiday Inn Express provides valuable insights into the morning routines and sleep habits of people in the UK and Ireland, and highlights the importance of understanding guest needs and preferences in the hospitality industry.
