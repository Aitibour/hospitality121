The Premier Lacrosse League and Women's Lacrosse League have announced a new partnership with World of Hyatt, naming the hospitality brand as the official sponsor for the 2026 season. This partnership is expected to elevate the fan experience and provide exclusive benefits to league players and staff. As part of the agreement, Hyatt will offer special rates and packages to fans and families attending lacrosse games. The company will also work with the leagues to create unique experiences, such as behind-the-scenes tours and meet-and-greets with players. This partnership highlights the growing intersection of sports and hospitality, with brands seeking to create immersive experiences for fans. The deal is also a strategic move for Hyatt, as it looks to expand its reach and appeal to a new demographic. The partnership is expected to have a positive impact on the lacrosse community, with increased exposure and support for the sport. Overall, the collaboration between Hyatt and the lacrosse leagues is a win-win for both parties, offering benefits for fans, players, and the hospitality brand alike.
