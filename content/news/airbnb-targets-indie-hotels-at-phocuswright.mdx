Report
Airbnb announced a new outreach aimed at independent hotels during the Phocuswright conference in Hollywood, Florida on November 18, 2026. The company described the initiative as a direct effort to court independent properties, positioning the move as a contrast to its largest rival, Booking.com. Lou Zameryka, Airbnb’s global head of hotel enterprise and connectivity partnerships, said that Booking.com is not hotel‑centric. The presentation confirmed that Airbnb is actively seeking to engage independent hotels through this new pitch.
