The **hospitality industry** has witnessed a significant change with the departure of Patrick Pacious as the CEO of Choice Hotels. After nearly nine years at the helm, Pacious is stepping down, and Dominic Dragisich will take over as the interim CEO. This sudden change in leadership has raised questions about the future of the company and its **franchising strategy**.


The **hotel franchising** landscape is highly competitive, and Choice Hotels has been a major player in this sector. With a large portfolio of brands, the company has been able to maintain its position in the market. However, the **travel industry** is constantly evolving, and companies must adapt to these changes to remain competitive. The new leadership will need to navigate these challenges and ensure that Choice Hotels remains a leader in the **hospitality sector**.


The appointment of Dominic Dragisich as the interim CEO is a significant development. As the **Chief Growth & Strategy Officer**, Dragisich has been responsible for driving the company's growth and strategy. His experience and knowledge of the company's operations will be crucial in this transition period. However, the fact that no permanent successor has been named raises questions about the company's long-term plans.


The **hospitality industry** is known for its high turnover rate, and leadership changes are not uncommon. However, the sudden departure of a CEO can have a significant impact on the company's operations and **employee morale**. It is essential for the new leadership to communicate effectively with employees and stakeholders to ensure a smooth transition.


The future of Choice Hotels will depend on the company's ability to adapt to the changing **travel landscape**. The new leadership will need to focus on **innovation** and **customer experience** to remain competitive. The company's **franchising model** has been successful in the past, but it may need to be adjusted to meet the changing needs of **hotel owners** and **travelers**.
