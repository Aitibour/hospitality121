The World Hotel Congress returns to Dubai in October, convening over 800 senior hospitality professionals from across the Middle East, Africa, and South Asia alongside delegates from Europe, North America, and the Asia-Pacific region.

Dubai's position as the world's most visited city and the premier hub for luxury hospitality in the Gulf makes it the natural host for a conference of this ambition. The 2026 edition coincides with a period of extraordinary development activity across the region — Vision 2030, NEOM, the Red Sea Project, and the continued expansion of UAE hotel capacity all provide material for what promises to be the most substantive programme in the Congress's seven-year history.

The conference programme spans three days and covers the full breadth of hotel business: investment and development, operations and technology, people and culture, sustainability and ESG, and the commercial strategies shaping revenue performance in competitive markets.

This year's headline theme — "Building for the Next Generation" — encompasses both physical development and the workforce and leadership development that will determine which organisations flourish in the next decade.

Speakers confirmed include regional presidents of three major international hotel groups, the chief executives of two of the region's leading real estate developers, and the directors-general of tourism boards across Gulf Cooperation Council member states.

The Congress's structured networking programme — including curated boardroom briefings, investor-operator roundtables, and an invitation-only gala dinner — is designed to facilitate the conversations and relationships that generate real commercial outcomes.

The World Hotel Congress is the premier gathering for hospitality professionals operating across or interested in the Middle East and wider region. Applications are reviewed; early registration is strongly recommended.
