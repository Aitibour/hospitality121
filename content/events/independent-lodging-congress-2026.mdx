The Independent Lodging Congress is the premier gathering for independent hotel owners, operators, and the advisors who serve them. Unlike the major branded hotel conferences, ILC is built explicitly around the interests and challenges of operators who have chosen to compete on distinctiveness rather than scale — and who understand that the economics of independence require a different set of skills, tools, and relationships.

Nashville's emergence as a hospitality destination in its own right makes it a fitting host for the 2026 edition. The city's independent hotel community has grown rapidly, driven by the demand for authentic local experiences that branded chains cannot replicate, and the ILC programme draws extensively on the lessons of operators who have built successful independent properties in competitive markets.

Sessions address distribution strategy for independents, the brand versus independent decision at asset transitions, direct booking conversion optimisation, the technology stack appropriate for properties without the resources of a major flag, and the community-building practices that drive the repeat guest relationships on which independent economics depend. The ILC is deliberately smaller than the major investment conferences — the intimacy of scale is itself a product feature.
