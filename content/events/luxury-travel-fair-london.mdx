The Luxury Travel and Hospitality Fair returns to London in December for its annual celebration of the finest in global travel — a curated gathering that brings together the world's leading luxury hotels, travel operators, and hospitality innovators in one of the world's great cities.

The Fair is designed for two audiences: the trade professionals — travel advisors, concierge teams, corporate travel managers, and lifestyle managers — who need a comprehensive and curated view of the luxury hospitality landscape; and the discerning travellers who want direct access to the properties and experiences that define the highest standards in the industry.

Over two days, exhibitors representing over 200 luxury hotel properties, private villa collections, yacht charter operators, private aviation companies, and curated experience providers invite visitors into their world through immersive presentations, tastings, destination showcases, and personalised consultations.

The trade day on December 2nd is exclusively for travel industry professionals, offering structured briefings, partnership meetings, and intimate roundtables with property representatives in a format designed to build the relationships that underpin the luxury travel ecosystem.

London's position in December — festive, atmospheric, and at the heart of the world's most active luxury travel source market — provides a backdrop that enhances every conversation. The Fair's evening programme, including a gala dinner at a landmark London venue, extends the commercial conversation into a genuinely memorable social experience.

Exhibitor places are limited; properties interested in showcasing at the Fair should register their interest through the H1H Events team. Trade visitor registration is complimentary for qualified travel professionals.
