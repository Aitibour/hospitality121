The Asia Pacific Hotel Summit convenes in Singapore this October, bringing together 500 hotel owners, operators, investors, and technology leaders for the region's most focused gathering of hospitality decision-makers.

Singapore's role as Asia's premier business hub, and one of the world's great hospitality cities in its own right, makes it the natural home for a Summit that spans the extraordinary diversity of the Asia-Pacific market: from Japan's ryokan culture to Australia's luxury resort developments, from India's scale opportunity to Southeast Asia's emergence as a global tourism powerhouse.

The 2026 Summit programme is structured around five themes that reflect the region's specific opportunity and challenge: capturing the growth of Asian middle-class travel, navigating talent shortages in markets with diverse cultural expectations, deploying technology in service-focused cultures, developing sustainable operations at scale across diverse regulatory environments, and attracting international investment to properties in emerging markets.

Keynote speakers confirmed for the 2026 Summit include the head of Asia-Pacific for one of the world's three largest hotel groups, the CEO of Singapore Tourism Board, the founder of a pan-Asian hotel investment platform managing over $2 billion in assets, and a leading authority on the intersection of digital technology and Asian consumer behaviour in travel.

Structured networking, including morning group sessions matched by seniority and functional focus, and a curated evening programme including a Singapore hawker market experience designed exclusively for Summit attendees, ensures that the relationships formed during the event extend beyond the conference hall.

Registrations are now open. Early registration is recommended; the 2025 Summit reached capacity six weeks before the event.
