The Restaurants Canada Show is Canada's premier food service and hospitality trade event, bringing together more than 15,000 industry professionals over three days at the Metro Toronto Convention Centre. The show spans every segment of Canada's foodservice sector, from independent restaurants and hotel F&B operations to institutional catering and quick-service chains.

The 2026 edition places particular emphasis on the intersection of technology and hospitality — including AI-assisted menu engineering, integrated reservation and point-of-sale systems, and the data infrastructure that allows operators to move from intuition-based to evidence-based decision-making. For hotel food and beverage teams, the show provides unmatched access to supplier innovation across equipment, ingredients, and operational software.

The Canadian Food and Beverage Awards ceremony takes place on the final evening, recognising operators, chefs, and suppliers who have demonstrated exceptional standards and innovation in the preceding twelve months. The networking events associated with the show consistently rank among the most productive in the Canadian hospitality calendar.
