Milan hosts the Hospitality F&B Innovation Expo this November — a two-day exhibition and conference programme dedicated to the people, ideas, and products transforming food and beverage in hotels, resorts, and hospitality venues globally.

The Expo brings together over 300 exhibitors — culinary technology providers, sustainable ingredient suppliers, beverage innovators, kitchen equipment manufacturers, and F&B concept developers — with an attendee audience of hotel food and beverage directors, executive chefs, and hospitality investors seeking the next generation of dining concepts.

The conference programme running alongside the exhibition floor features chefs from Michelin-starred hotel restaurants sharing the strategic and culinary thinking behind their programmes, food and beverage directors from major hotel groups discussing how to build dining destinations within hotel environments, and investors and developers explaining what they look for when evaluating F&B propositions for new hotel projects.

Milan's position as one of the world's great food cities adds a dimension that no other Expo location could replicate. Attendees have access to an exclusive programme of evening dinners at landmark restaurants, curated market visits at dawn, and producer visits in the surrounding Lombardy region — experiences designed to feed creative thinking that will shape menus and concepts for years to come.

The Expo's Innovation Stage showcases early-stage F&B concepts seeking hospitality partners and pilots, offering operators a first look at ideas that may define hotel dining in the next decade.

Registration is open to hotel F&B professionals, chefs, investors, and industry suppliers. H1H community members receive a preferential registration rate and priority access to the Innovation Stage showcases.
